What each type means
feature
New or changed behaviour that a user of that part of the product sees or uses.
bug
Something that should work, or used to work, and doesn't.
chore
A change that increases value and helps the product work better, but isn't visible to its users.
CRM users
Sales reps, managers and the customer's admins, in the app or its settings. Developers calling the API don't count.
orchestrator users
The engineering team, through its comments, labels, checks and dashboards. What runs behind those is a chore.
